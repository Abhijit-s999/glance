    You'll read a few short passages, one word at a time, and answer one question about each. After every passage, choose slower or faster. The steps get smaller each time you change direction, so you settle on a speed that feels easy and still lets you catch the details.

    Keep your eyes on the red letter. It sits where a word is recognised fastest, so your eyes never have to move.
